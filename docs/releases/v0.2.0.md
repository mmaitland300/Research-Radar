# v0.2.0 Curated Surface

Date: 2026-06-13

Intended tag: `v0.2.0`

## Status

This release marks a curation milestone for Research Radar. The project now
reads more clearly as a solo research recommender prototype: product code and
concise docs on `main`, heavy experiment records archived, and current evidence
limits stated plainly.

It is not a validated recommender claim. The ranking surfaces remain useful for
exploration and engineering review, not proof of relevance quality.

## What Changed

- Historical ML governance and process artifacts moved out of the public
  critical path.
- `docs/audit/` was slimmed to runtime-required frozen artifacts, with
  `docs/audit/README.md` explaining why each artifact remains on `main`.
- An internal retrospective was added to explain why the shadow/governance
  experiment happened, what it taught, and why that process was discontinued.
- The pipeline CLI was split into family-scoped parser and dispatch modules
  while preserving the command surface.
- FastAPI routes were split into domain routers while preserving the route
  method/path surface.
- Alembic revision `0002` and snapshot-membership plumbing are on `main`, so
  composed corpus snapshots can reference works without moving canonical rows.

## Known Limits

- Research Radar is not a validated recommender system.
- Evaluation remains proxy, single-reviewer, and limited unless a specific
  future release note says otherwise.
- Bridge recommendation evidence remains experimental.
- `apps/web/app/recommended/page.tsx` still needs a smaller component split.
- Frontend tests remain future cleanup.
- Python dependency locking and reproducibility hygiene remain future cleanup.

## Where The Full Experiment Record Lives

The full ML governance and rollout-review record lives on the
`archive/ml-governance-audit` branch. `main` keeps product code, curated docs,
and the frozen artifacts that deployed scorers load or hash-pin at runtime.
